# 🚗 Road Accident Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes road accident data to identify key patterns, high-risk factors, and casualty trends across different time periods, vehicle types, and environmental conditions. The goal is to provide actionable, data-driven insights to help improve road safety and traffic management.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** [e.g., Excel / SQL / Python (Pandas)]
* **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** [e.g., Power BI / Tableau / Excel]

## 📊 Key KPIs Tracked
* **Total Casualties & Accidents:** [e.g., 417,883 total casualties]
* **Casualty Severity Breakdown:** Fatal, Serious, and Slight casualties
* **Monthly & Yearly Trends:** Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison and peak accident months
* **Casualties by Vehicle Type:** Breakdown across cars, motorcycles, buses, vans, etc.
* **Environmental & Road Factors:** Impact of weather conditions, road surface, light conditions (day vs. night), and urban vs. rural areas

## 🔍 Key Insights
* **Peak Timeframes:** [e.g., Accidents peaked in October and November, with lowest numbers in January and February.]
* **Vehicle Impact:** [e.g., Cars accounted for ~80% of total casualties.]
* **Road & Light Conditions:** [e.g., Most accidents occurred on single carriageways, dry road surfaces, and during daylight hours in urban areas.]
* **Severity:** [e.g., Over 84% of casualties were slight, while fatal casualties accounted for 1.7%.]

## 💡 Recommendations
* Increase traffic monitoring and awareness campaigns during peak accident months.
* Focus infrastructure improvements and speed enforcement on high-risk road types (e.g., single carriageways and urban intersections).
